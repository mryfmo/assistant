# 09_review — Review Focus

## Review What Matters

- Is behavior boundary preserved?
- Is change intent clear from diff?
- Is evidence sufficient and reproducible?
- Is rollback realistic?

## Review What Not To Overweight

- Personal style preferences when standards are already satisfied.
- Large architectural debate in a small refactor-only patch.

## Required Review Artifacts

- Boundary statement
- Verification command output
- Risk note and mitigation
